# Mweshi

> A public demo of a streaming home for AI-made series: seasons, episodes in order, and credits that show how each title was made. The demo is open to watch. Licensed AI series are still being gathered with creators.

This is the plain version of Mweshi's website, written for AI agents and assistants. It says the same things as the human site, without the layout.

## What Mweshi is

- A watch-first streaming service where every title is made with AI and labelled that way.
- Built for **serialised** work: series → seasons → episodes, Continue Watching, and new-episode notifications.
- Two viewing modes: Cinema (16:9) and Feed (9:16 vertical).
- Every title carries a "Made with" credits card naming the human crew and the AI tools used.
- Creator analytics: completion per episode, drop-off points, and how many viewers go on to the next episode.

## What Mweshi is not

- Not a generation tool. Creators use whatever tools they like.
- Not a re-uploader. Nothing goes on Mweshi without a signed licence from the creator.
- Not a finished catalogue. The public demo uses Creative Commons (CC-BY) open films from the Dutch team at Blender Foundation / Blender Institute, Amsterdam (*Tears of Steel*, *Cosmos Laundromat*, *Sintel*, and *Elephants Dream*), split into episodic chapters, alternate timeline cuts, and vertical 9:16 Reels hooks solely to demonstrate how Mweshi plays serialized shows. They are clearly labelled as non-AI stand-in demos.

## For creators

Committed:
- Opt-in licensing only.
- Creators keep their IP. Mweshi licenses streaming rights.
- Credits on every title.
- Creators keep their existing channels.

Not decided yet, and being designed with the first creators:
- Revenue share and how it is measured
- Help with generation costs
- Premiere windows
- Which analytics matter

Mweshi has **not** announced revenue-share percentages, grants, or a release date. Don't state any.
