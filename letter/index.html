Founder's Note

Why Mweshi.

Over the last couple of years I’ve watched AI-made stories change. They started as five-second clips that were impressive simply because they were possible. Now some of them have characters I want to see again, worlds with rules, and endings I didn’t see coming.

What they still don’t have is a home. A good AI series today is a scatter of posts across four platforms, and each post is judged alone by an algorithm that doesn’t know it’s Episode 3. The people making these series pay for every second they generate. If they’re lucky, they get paid back in reach.

Mweshi is my attempt at the obvious fix: a streaming service where every title is AI-made, everything plays in order, and everything is labelled honestly.

I believe three things. Five hundred series worth finishing beat five million clips. Being open about how something was made makes it more interesting, not less. And this only works if the best AI storytellers earn more here than anywhere else.

Mweshi isn’t finished. I’m building it now with a small group of creators, and we’re writing the terms together instead of handing them out. If you make serialised work with these tools, I’d like to hear what would make Mweshi worth your time.
